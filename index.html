Strategy first. Systems always. Execution until it ships.

I work upstream - shaping what gets built before anyone opens a design tool. I ask the business question before the UX question, and I stay in until the feature is live and working.

Over the past two years I've integrated AI into the design process - cutting research synthesis from days to hours, accelerating documentation, and improving design-to-engineering handoffs.

  • 01

    Product thinking

    I ask "should we build this?" before "how should this look?" I work with founders, PMs and engineering leads to define what matters and what doesn't.

  • 02

    Systems at scale

    I build design systems that compound over time - not just component libraries, but the foundations, tokens, and governance that let teams move faster as the product grows.

  • 03

    Outcomes, not outputs

    My measure of success is whether the product works for real users - not whether the prototype impressed in review. I stay in until the thing ships.
